# Status Reporting Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Whenever the user asks for "status" or inquiries about performance:
You MUST provide an exhaustive, fill-by-fill accounting report containing:
1. **Direct Answer to "Are we in profit or loss?"**:
   - Total Net Account PnL since starting ($9.12 -> $7.53).
   - Post-fix Disciplined PnL (Wins vs Controlled Losses).
2. **Table of All Trades**:
   - Symbol & Date/Time
   - Outcome (PROFIT / LOSS)
   - Exact Dollar Amount ($ USD)
   - Exact Exit Reason ("WHY" it gained or lost: target hit, trailing stop, thesis invalidation scratch, support breakdown, or legacy multiplier discrepancy).
3. **Current Live State**:
   - Current Capital & Margin Availability
   - Open Positions with unrealized PnL and active stop-loss/take-profit brackets
   - Progress toward the Wednesday Target ($25.00 USD).
